Command Center

A personal daily assistant that runs on one home computer, for one family.

Command Center is a private, self-hosted tool. Each morning it puts together a short spoken briefing (calendar, reminders, local news, school notices) and shows the same information on a web page that only the owner's own devices can reach. It is not a public service: nobody can sign up for it, and it has no users other than its owner.

Why it signs in to Google

It reads the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and one Google Drive folder so the briefing can mention today's events, emails the owner sent to himself as reminders, and voice notes recorded on his watch. It can also manage two calendars that it creates itself. It cannot send email, and it does not change or delete anything in Gmail or Drive other than adding one label to emails it has already read out.

See the privacy policy for exactly what it reads and where that data stays.
